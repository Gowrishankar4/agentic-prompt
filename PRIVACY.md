# Privacy

Agentic Prompt collects no data and sends nothing anywhere.

- **What it reads:** your request, and your project's files when it reads the code before interviewing you. This happens on your machine, inside your Claude session.
- **What it writes:** the approved spec — shown in the conversation, or saved as a markdown file where you choose — and an optional config file. Nothing else.
- **What it sends:** nothing. The plugin has no server, no analytics, no telemetry and no network calls of its own. Your conversation with Claude is covered by your Claude account's terms and privacy policy.
- **Sensitive data:** if you paste secrets or personal data, the skill asks you to redact them before continuing, and keeps them out of specs.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Gowrishankar4/agentic-prompt/issues.
